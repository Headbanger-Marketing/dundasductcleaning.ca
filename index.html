Naming indoor dust and soot sources before attributing them to the ducts

Note what you know first-hand about where dust or dark marks appear in your Dundas home, how soon they return after wiping, what happens in that room and when, and whether the supply registers show them first. You may be unsure whether candles, a wood stove, cooking, renovation, pets or outdoor traffic through the doors cause them, or whether the ducts have anything to do with them. Mark which is which in your request, and the responding provider can then separate household sources from anything worth checking inside the ducts.

  1. Return grilles

    Where room air is drawn back toward the furnace.

  2. Return ducts

    Carry that air back to the furnace.

  3. Furnace blower and coil

    The blower, evaporator coil and cabinet.

  4. Supply trunk

    The main duct leaving the furnace.

  5. Branch runs

    Smaller ducts feeding each room.

  6. Supply registers

    The floor, wall or ceiling vents in each room.

Separate runs to ask about:Dryer ventHRV core and screensBathroom fan ductsRange hood exhaust

  • Where the marks appear

    Name each room and surface, and say whether dust or dark film sits at a supply register, on shelves or near a doorway.

  • Activity near those spots

    List candles, a stove, cooking, pets, renovation work and door traffic, with the rooms and times they happen.

  • How soon marks return

    Say how long a surface stays clear after wiping, and whether that changes when the household activity stops for a while.

Questions About Your Dundas Request

What sort of site is Dundas Duct Cleaning, and does it have its own crew?

As an enquiry website rather than a contractor, Dundas Duct Cleaning does not do the work or set its terms. It has no office, crew, vehicles or equipment behind it. Our enquiry team reviews each request and may put it before a provider. Before anything is arranged or any price is discussed, any provider it goes to must identify themselves and confirm they cover your address.

What happens to my Dundas request after I press send?

Sending a request first places your description in front of our enquiry team for review. A provider may then respond, and if one does, they must introduce themselves, confirm they can reach your address and set out scope, price and timing in writing before you decide anything. The request itself does not book work, fix a price, confirm coverage or name a provider, and only after you agree is anything arranged.

Which household sources should I list before asking about dust in Dundas?

Candles, a wood stove or gas stove, cooking, renovation work, pets, open doors and a nearby laundry area are the usual ones to name. Add the room, the time of day and how soon the marks return after wiping. A list like that lets a provider separate ordinary sources from anything worth looking at inside the ducts. Photos taken at the same spot on different days also help.

Can I send a Dundas request if I have no dust or soot concern?

Yes. The dust and soot list is one example of what a useful request contains. Any duct, dryer vent, furnace and AC, HRV, sanitizing, sealing or commercial need can be sent, with the rooms, the systems involved and whatever photos you already have. Describe what you noticed and what you want to ask. Our enquiry team reads each one the same way.

Does dust at one register mean my whole duct system is dirty?

No single register shows the state of the whole system. A dusty grille may only reflect room dust that settled there, so ask the provider what they observed at several registers and what they could not reach. Photos of clean and marked registers side by side make that comparison easier to discuss. Ask which registers the provider checked before any wider work is proposed.

When could work happen at my Dundas house after I send a request?

This site does not set a date for any visit. Our enquiry team reviews the request first, and timing is then settled between you and the provider who responds. Ask that provider for the earliest time they can offer, in writing, together with the scope and the price, and compare it with your own plans before agreeing.

What should I do if I notice a gas smell or suspect carbon monoxide?

Leave the building and contact the gas supplier or emergency responders. Do not wait for an enquiry response. Questions about fuel, combustion, refrigerant or electrical controls need appropriately qualified diagnosis, and they sit outside a duct cleaning request, so keep them apart from anything you send here. Even if the smell has passed, raise it with the gas supplier rather than waiting for a duct cleaning visit.

Who decides whether work goes ahead after my request?

You decide, along with anyone who can authorize work on the property, such as a landlord, a property manager or a condominium corporation. A provider can only propose a written scope. Ask the provider to separate agreed work from extra findings, and approve nothing on the spot. Keep any written notes with your request.
